# PartyList

PartyList is an Android app that helps you keep track of the birthdays of the
people who matter to you. It brings names, dates, and photos together in one
place, so you can see upcoming celebrations without relying on memory.

Browse birthdays in a list or calendar, schedule notifications, and personalize
the app with your preferred theme, accent color, and language. PartyList keeps
your birthday list on your device and lets you back it up or transfer selected
people using a `.partylist` file.

## Features

### Keep your people in one place

Add someone manually or choose a contact from your phone. Give each person a
photo, crop it to fit, and save their birthday with or without a birth year.

Search the list, sort it alphabetically or by upcoming birthday, and select
multiple people when you need to remove entries together. Each entry shows how
many days remain until the next birthday.

### Explore birthdays in the calendar

Browse birthdays by month or switch to a compact view of the year. Select any
day to see who is celebrating, including when several people share a birthday.

Tap a person in the calendar to find them in the list, or return to today's date
with a single button.

### Schedule birthday notifications

Choose how many days before a birthday notifications should begin and how often
they should repeat. You can set one or more specific times or use an hourly
interval, depending on how you prefer to be notified.

### Personalize the app

- Choose between light and dark themes.
- Adjust the accent color with the color palette.
- Select the white or dark app icon.
- Choose from 50 language options, including Romanian, English, German, French,
  Italian, Bulgarian, Turkish, Chinese, Japanese, and Russian.

### Back up and transfer your list

Export a single person, a selection of people, or the complete list to a
`.partylist` file. When importing, you can add the people from the file to your
existing list or replace the existing list after confirmation.

Exported files can contain names, birth dates, and photos, so keep them somewhere
safe and share them only with people you trust.
