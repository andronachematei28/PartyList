# Privacy

PartyList stores its birthday list and settings on the device. Contact access is used when adding a person from contacts; notification permission enables scheduled birthday notifications. Availability and behavior can depend on Android and device settings.

A `.partylist` export can contain names, birth dates, and photographs. Treat exported files as private and share them only with people you trust. Importing with the replace option removes the existing list after confirmation.

This repository does not include personal birthday lists, exported backups, private signing keys, or user photographs.
