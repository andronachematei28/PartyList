## AI assistance attribution

When an AI assistant materially contributes to a change, identify that assistance in the commit description or pull request. For example, when OpenAI Codex was used:

`AI-assisted-by: OpenAI Codex`

Do not claim that an AI system owns PartyList or has a verified GitHub contributor account. Do not invent a `Co-authored-by` identity or email. Keep product ownership and final decisions attributed to andronachematei28. Do not add AI attribution to the application interface.
